I tend to post pictures of what I'm working on over on my instagram <a href="https://www.instagram.com/rpf_morrison/">@rpf_morrison</a> but I've been thinking I'd like to be able to post more wordy updates from time to time. Slightly more permanent than a social media post, but less academic than trying to write for the BVMA or VDGS newsletters. That's it, really. It's not going to be regular or frequent, but sometimes I might write a bit about what I'm doing, or planning, or thinking.
