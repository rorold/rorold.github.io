I am currently developing my own mandolin design, with a bent spruce soundboard and cylindrical flat back based on some of the construction methods I used for my octave mandolin and bass viol.

It will have a larger body and slightly longer scale length than the standard gibson-style blugrass mandolins, giving it a more mellow tone with plenty of bass response. The bent front should also give it good volume and treble response, hopefully combining to give a good balanced sound that projects well.
