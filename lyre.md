<p>I designed this Lyre based partly on the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buste-socle">Buste-Socle figurine</a> the Bard of Paule from 2nd Century BCE Brittany, along with other iron-age representations of lyres, and with some elements taken from later Saxon lyres. It is an attempt at a plausible instrument to match archaeological finds from iron-age Scotland which have been identified as lyre bridges.</p>
